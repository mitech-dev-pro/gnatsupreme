Global Settings

Cover amounts — same for every member on the scheme
Every enrolled member automatically has all four benefits below — nothing is optional or picked per member. The enrolled member is the Main Life: if the Main Life dies, the Member amount pays out; if their named Spouse dies, the Spouse amount pays out. There's one flat monthly premium for the whole package. Only miLife (National) can change these.

Data Access & Privacy

Handled under ISO 27001 and Ghana's Data Protection Act. A member's benefit, premium, spouse, and beneficiary details are visible only to that member, to miLife staff, and to anyone explicitly granted access below — district and regional admins only ever see members within their own scope.
